Playboi Carti rose from Atlanta's underground scene with a style built on hypnotic ad-libs, minimalist flows, and atmospheric production that redefined what hip-hop could sound like.

Beyond music, he became a cultural force — fusing punk attitude, gothic fashion, and internet mystique into a persona that influenced an entire generation of artists, designers, and fans worldwide.

The Journey

2015
Awful Records Era
Signed to Father's Awful Records. Released early underground hits like "Broke Boi" and "Fetti", establishing his repetitive, catchy Atlanta trap sound.
2016
ASAP Mob Affiliation
Signed to A$AP Mob's AWGE imprint and Interscope Records. Featured on the Cozy Tapes compilation, elevating his status in the fashion-rap crossover scene.
2017
Self-Titled & "Magnolia"
Released his eponymous debut mixtape featuring the massive hit "Magnolia". The Pi'erre Bourne produced track became his mainstream breakthrough.
2018—2020
Die Lit to WLR
Dropped the critically acclaimed "Die Lit", followed by a massive aesthetic shift and the highly polarizing, culturally resetting "Whole Lotta Red".
2021—Present
Opium & I AM MUSIC
Founded the Opium label, shaping the next generation of underground rap (Ken Carson, Destroy Lonely). Preparing for his third studio album rollout.

ERAS

2018

DIE LIT

Focused on high-energy beats and repetitive flows, creating a hypnotic, punk-infused masterpiece.

This era defined modern trap, turning moshpits into a staple of underground hip-hop culture. Carti's stage dives became legendary — fueled by Pi'erre Bourne's ethereal production.

2020

WHOLE LOTTA RED

A polarizing, aggressive shift introducing the punk-inspired rage sound, distorted synths, and screaming vocals.

The birth of the "Vamp" aesthetic. High contrast, blood red, and chaotic structure completely shifted the meta. What was once hated became the blueprint.

2024–2025

I AM MUSIC

Deeper voices, ambient soundscapes, and a more elusive, mysterious persona. The chaotic rage evolves into calculated darkness.

Industrial textures, lo-fi aesthetics, and cold minimalism. Carti steps fully into the role of a fashion-forward, untouchable figure.

EMPIRE

OPIUM LABEL

Carti's imprint is more than a record label; it's an aesthetic movement. Opium artists share the same gothic, high-fashion sensibilities and distorted sonic palettes.

Cultural Impact

Rage Music Pioneer

Carti didn't just participate in the rage subgenre — he created the template. WLR's chaotic beats and screaming delivery became the DNA of artists like Ken Carson, Destroy Lonely, and an entire SoundCloud wave. His ad-lib style ("What?", "SLATT") became a universal language in modern hip-hop.

Fashion & Youth Culture

He blurred the line between streetwear and high fashion. His relationship with Rick Owens, Raf Simons, and Balenciaga made underground designer labels cool to Gen-Z. The all-black, oversized, goth-punk look became the uniform of a generation raised on TikTok and SoundCloud.

Luxury & Brand Power

Carti transcended music into a fashion icon: Givenchy campaigns, Virgil Abloh collaborations, Paris Fashion Week front rows. His label Opium is a brand itself — a symbol of elite, mysterious, anti-mainstream energy that luxury houses now chase for cultural relevance.
